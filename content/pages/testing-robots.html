Customizable Testing Robots

Our testing robots exercise mobile phones, applications, and embedded devices without installing any test framework, agent, or client software on the device under test.

  • Smartphones, tablets, and mobile applications

  • Production-line checks and repeatable regression scenarios
  • Scenarios tailored to your product, interfaces, and acceptance criteria

Why It Matters

Conventional automation often depends on privileged access, rooted devices, or pre-installed test clients. That limits coverage, slows rollout, and is often impossible in secure or sealed environments.

Our approach removes that dependency. You can validate firmware builds, UI flows, and device behaviour on real hardware - including environments where changing the device configuration is not permitted.
